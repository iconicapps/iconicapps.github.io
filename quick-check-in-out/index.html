About

What is Quick Check In Out?

Quick Check In Out turns an iPad, iPhone or Android tablet into a simple sign-in station for daycares, preschools and children's programs. Parents find their child by the first letter of their name, tap check in or check out, and sign on the screen. Every entry is time-stamped and saved with the parent's signature.

Staff get a PIN-protected admin area to manage children, review who hasn't been checked in or out, correct entries, and export a printable daily attendance log as a PDF.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A to Z child finder

Parents pick a letter and tap their child's name, by first or last name.

Parent signatures

Each check-in and check-out is signed on screen and time-stamped.

Daily attendance

See at a glance who hasn't been checked in or checked out.

PDF daily log

Export, print or share a day's attendance with signatures.

PIN-protected admin

Settings, edits and reports stay behind an admin PIN.

Optional parent history

Let parents view recent check-ins, for the period you choose.

Privacy first

Children's information stays with you

Quick Check In Out was built for places that look after children. It works without the internet, and nothing you enter leaves the device unless you choose to export or share it.

Read the privacy policy
  • All data stays on your device, with no accounts and no cloud.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Iconic Apps never receives any information about children or families.
  • The camera and photo library are used only to add a child's profile photo.
