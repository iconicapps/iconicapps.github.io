// What is it

A business card, written for AI.

An agent card is a small, machine-readable file published on your website, usually at /.well-known/agent-card.json. It's part of the open Agent2Agent (A2A) standard, which lets AI agents from different companies find and talk to each other.

When a customer's AI assistant goes looking for a dentist, a caterer or a contractor, it reads your card to learn what you can do and exactly how to ask you to do it. No guessing from web pages.

  • identity Who you are and who runs the agent
  • skills The tasks agents can ask you to do
  • endpoint Where agents send their requests
  • security How agents must sign in, and what they can access
