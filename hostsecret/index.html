About

What is Host Secret?

Host Secret is a local secret generation tool designed to quickly create temporary secrets, codes, or random strings for immediate use. It's ideal for generating quick reference codes, temporary access tokens, or non-sensitive identifiers.

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, synced to the cloud, or shared with third parties.

Host Secret is not a password manager. Don't use it to store banking, email or social media passwords, or any other sensitive information.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Turn a phrase you choose into a key.

PIN based

Generate a key from a numeric PIN.

Random characters

Local only
